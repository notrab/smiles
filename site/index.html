Three ways to ask

Put the question where the experience actually happens.

Tab

Sits in the corner of every page, ready when someone has an opinion. Best for a running read on the whole product.

Inline

Drop it next to one thing, a checkout or an article, and find out how that one thing lands.

Email

Three faces at the foot of a support reply. One click answers the question, with no login and no form.

Answers land where you work

Every answer, or only the unhappy ones. Your call, per destination.

Email

Through Resend. Connect the account, no API key to paste.

Slack

A message in the channel you pick, the moment it arrives.

Discord

An embed coloured by mood, in any channel webhook.

Webhook

Your own endpoint, with an HMAC signature to check.
