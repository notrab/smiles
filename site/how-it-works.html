How it works

One question, one click to answer, one number to watch.

1

Write the question

One question, in your words. "How was your checkout?" "Did that answer help?" Short questions get answered. Surveys get closed.

2

Paste one script tag

Your site key is already in it. Add ?tab=1 for the corner tab, or drop a div anywhere you want the question inline.

<script src="https://your-widget-host/your-key.js?tab=1" async></script>

<!-- or inline, wherever you want it -->
<div data-smiles data-question="How was your checkout?"></div>
3

People answer with a face

Awesome, just OK, not good. That is the whole survey. Anyone who wants to say more gets a comment box after they pick, and most will not bother, which is fine.

4

You get a score, and the answers

Your score is the share of answers that were awesome. Every answer also goes wherever you point it: email, Slack, Discord, or your own webhook. Ship a change, watch the number, follow up on the unhappy ones the same day.

Two minutes to install

Then you never have to wonder how it is going.
